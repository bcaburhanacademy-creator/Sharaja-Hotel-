Our Heritage & Passion

A Beloved Culinary Landmark in Yaru, Pishin

Sharjah Hotel Yaru is a renowned local Pakistani restaurant proudly serving travelers, families, and food lovers along the National Highway in Yaru, District Pishin, Balochistan.

From fresh tender mutton cooked in natural tallow to fiery iron-wok chicken karahis and fragrant biryani, we honor authentic roadside culinary traditions with genuine warmth, generous portions, and unmatched freshness.

  • ✔ Fresh slaughtered meat & live iron-wok cooking
  • ✔ Special late-night service closing at 2:00 AM
  • ✔ Welcoming mountain hospitality for every guest
